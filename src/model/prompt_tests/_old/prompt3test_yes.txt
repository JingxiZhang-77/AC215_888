A patient was receiving care in an OB Clinic. This patient had a history of insulin dependent gestational diabetes. Although the clinic did instruct the patient in how to care for the problem, she was known to have some behavioral health and social issues and was inconsistent in self-care and didn’t always keep her visits. They discussed options to more safely care for this patient but hadn’t implemented a plan of care. Ultimately the patient delivered the infant via c section, but there was unfortunately a fetal demise.
